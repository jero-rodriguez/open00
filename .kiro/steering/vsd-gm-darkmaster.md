# VsD GM, Darkmaster & Rewards Reference

Mechanical reference for Against the Darkmaster (VsD) v1.5 Game Master tools, opponent creation, the Darkmaster, Battle & War, and rewards.
Use this when implementing NPC generation, campaign tools, or reward systems.

## GM Principles

1. Describe a living world (dynamic situations, small details)
2. Challenge the heroes (obstacles, hard choices, threaten resources)
3. Play by the rules (impartial, don't fudge rolls without group consent)
4. Spotlight Passions (tie scenarios to PC Passions)
5. Let heroes decide their fate (no forced outcomes, no illusory choices)
6. Make the game yours (adapt rules to group preferences)

## NPCs

### Secondary NPCs
- Improvised on the spot, few words of description
- Use NPC Quirks table (d100) for flavor
- May grow into Important NPCs over time

### Important NPCs
- Name, few sentences on motives/personality
- Single Passion (Motivation or Allegiance)
- Stats only if they'll enter conflict with PCs
- Use Kin/Culture descriptions for inspiration

### Opponents

Three tiers:

| Type | Stat Points | Level vs PCs | Special Abilities |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | None or single +5/+10 | Lower than PCs | 0 (or 1 from kin) |
| Elite | 25-40 distributed | Similar to PCs | 1-3 |
| Antagonist | 50-75 distributed | 2-3× PC level | Unlimited |

### Opponent Defeat Thresholds

**Common**: Flee/surrender if penalties >= half CMB or -50; Bruised; Severe Bleeding; or half their group disabled.

**Elite**: Flee if penalties >= half CMB or -75. Stay and fight even outnumbered/wounded if reasonable chance to win.

**Antagonist**: Fight to death only if motivated. GM dives into NPC personality.

### Opponents and Drive

- Common/Elite: never have Drive
- Antagonists: 1-3 points (use for re-rolling SRs or reducing Criticals, not replenished until story arc ends)

## Reaction Rolls

Open-Ended d100 (no skill bonus, small situational modifiers):

| Roll | Disposition | Interaction | Combat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 or less | Very Unfavourable | Won't listen, hostile or outrageous demands | Fight viciously to death |
| 01-25 | Unfavourable | Reluctant, higher prices | Aggressive, will attack |
| 26-75 | Neutral | Business-like, expects compensation | Cautious, defensive |
| 76-100 | Favourable | Helpful for small cost, some risk | Retreat if allowed, consider surrender |
| 101+ | Very Favourable | Loyal, suffers losses, follows into danger | Terrified, flee or surrender |

## The Darkmaster

### Creating the Darkmaster

Components to define:
1. **Epithet** - Name/title (e.g., The Horned King, The Witch Queen)
2. **Genesis** - Origin story
3. **Appearance & Symbol** - Visual form and heraldic mark
4. **Goals** - Nefarious plans
5. **Servants** - Lieutenants (powerful), Minions (bulk forces), Spies (recon)
6. **Dark Place** - Fortress/lair location
7. **Powers** - Mechanical effect on campaign
8. **Coveted Artifact** - Item tied to Darkmaster's fate

### Darkmaster Powers (Optional, choose one)

| Power | Effect |
|---|---|
| The Darkmaster (default) | No additional powers beyond base rules |
| Eldritch Horrors | WSR vs fear on first seeing abominations; use Fear Save Rolls Table |
| Dark Temptation | GM suggests easy way out; PCs can auto-succeed or auto-crit by accepting Taint |
| Devious Warmaster | Army Menace Rating +1; Elite/Antagonists declare actions after PCs |
| Life Scourge | Hunting/foraging yields half; Injured characters risk Barrow Rot or Bog Fever |
| Fimbulvinter | Normal→Rough, Rough→Arduous terrain; Power Rating grows, cumulative -10 Wandering; -100 total = world frozen |
| Sower of Discord | -20 Reaction Rolls vs different Kin/Culture; each PC has a Secret (extra Passion) |
| Stranger Dreams | Demons cast Disguise 1/day free; sleeping near Darkmaster symbols causes visions/Weary |

### Fear Save Rolls Table

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| -51 or less | Dead Scared (heart stops, literal death) |
| Up to -25 | Scared (frozen, swoon, or flee for scene) |
| Up to -1 | Shaken (-20 all actions while source in sight) |
| 0-50 | Brave (resist) |
| 51+ | Fearless (immune rest of scene, allies re-roll +20) |

### Taint

Gained by: learning Dark Spell Lore, using Tainted item, increasing Affinity with Tainted Item of Power, pacting with demon/Darkmaster.

Progression:
1. Motivation → Obsession
2. Allegiance → Dark Oath
3. Nature → Perversion (character becomes NPC)

Redemption: One chance only. Forgo source + heroic deed + spend Milestone to revert one Dark Passion.

### Coveted Artifact Examples

Items tied to both Darkmaster's triumph and destruction (Ring, Sword, Crown, Crystal, Cauldron, Spear, Amulet, Sceptre, Skull, Mirror).

### Defeating the Darkmaster

- Cannot be defeated by normal combat (no stats)
- Requires fulfilling prophecy, using Coveted Artifact, or similar epic quest
- Heroes rarely confront directly; they thwart plans

## Battle & War (Heroic Battles)

### Menace Rating

Represents enemy army strength vs heroes' side (1-10):
- 1-3: Roughly equal or slight advantage
- 4-6: Considerably stronger, needs brilliant plans
- 7+: Desperate, nearly hopeless without epic actions

### Resolution

Heroes make plans to reduce Menace Rating:

| Victory Type | MR Reduction | Typical Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | -1 | Challenging (-10) base |
| Moderate | -2 | Hard (-20) base |
| Epic | -3 | Extremely Hard (-40) base |

Examples of plans:
- Minor: arm/train villagers, set traps, recruit mercenaries
- Moderate: assassinate enemy commander, recruit allied clan, exploit terrain
- Epic: destroy enemy's greatest asset, gain powerful ally, eliminate war machine

### Outcome

- **MR reaches 0**: Heroes' side wins
- **MR not reduced**: Total defeat (captured/fleeing)
- **MR partially reduced (less than half)**: Enemy wins but suffers minor setback
- **MR reduced by half or more**: Enemy wins pyrrhic victory (heavy losses, failed objectives)

### Skills in Battle

Broaden skill scope for mass combat:
- Combat Skill = how well character fights with unit
- Charisma = bolster morale
- Healer = manage wounded
- Spell Casting = single roll for battle-long magical support

## Rewards

### Drive
- Most common reward
- At least one chance per PC per session
- Award during play = faster pace; award at session end = more cautious tone

### Experience Points
- Standard: via Achievement List (3-4 XPs/session)
- Bonus: 1-2 extra XPs for exceptional goals/moments (keep rare)

### Wealth (Treasure)
- Always has a story (origin, who owned it, why it's here)
- Treasure Value 1-5 compared to character WL
- World is quasi-medieval: wealth = land/status/trust, not just coins

### Alternative Rewards
- Needed items (rations, mounts, equipment)
- Favors, information, services
- Supernatural aid/blessings (raised Stats, lifted curses, spirit allies)
- Political status (titles, land, followers)

### Magic Items as Rewards
- Each should feel special and unique
- Name and backstory required
- Frequency depends on setting (standard VsD: fairly uncommon)
- Discovering new properties via Heroic Path is preferred over finding new items

## Campaign Pacing Tools

### Doom Clock (Optional)
- d10 visible to players, starts at 0
- Escalates on trigger (per round, per scene, per failure, etc.)
- Each escalation: increase die face by 1, then roll d10. If roll <= clock value: doom triggers.

### Safe Haven Limits
- 1 for single adventures/short campaigns
- 2-4 for longer campaigns
- Once all discovered, no new ones found

### Generational Play
- Old PCs retire, new Level 1 characters begin
- New gen gets extra Background Points (1 if old gen had <5 Milestones, 2 if >=5)
- Old PCs can appear as NPCs but never take active prominent role

### Conservative Progression (Beyond Level 10)
- Stop gaining XPs; only Heroic Path advances characters

### Continuous Progression (Beyond Level 10)
- Levels 11-15: 30 XPs per level
- Levels 16-20: 40 XPs per level
- And so on (+10 XPs per bracket of 5 levels)
